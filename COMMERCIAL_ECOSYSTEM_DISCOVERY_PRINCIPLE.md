# Reusable Commercial-Ecosystem Discovery Principle

Discover the addressable sales market through evidence of products sold and supplier relationships—not only names or directory categories. Model manufacturer/importer/dealer links as a graph; search category variants in Hebrew, Arabic and common transliterations; keep physical branches separate for field-sales use; prefer explicit high-recall tiers (CORE_RELEVANT, STRONG_ADJACENT, RELEVANCE_TO_VERIFY); and preserve uncertain records for later verification instead of silently excluding or merging them.

This is a reusable principle, not a finalized SOP.

## Supplier-customer lists

A photographed supplier customer list is a high-value discovery seed, not proof that a business is currently active, public, or uniquely identified. Preserve source filename and row provenance; exclude private/internal account identifiers; then cross-reference live and discovery data. Only public corroboration may supply contact details or justify promotion. Locality, spelling, branch, and stale-record conflicts remain visible review states rather than being silently merged.
